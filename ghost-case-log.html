Why open-weight?

Ghost Case Log calls llama-3.3-70b-versatile — Meta's open-weight model — through Groq's free tier. No subscription, no per-case fee, no vendor lock-in. Swap in any open model (Mixtral, Qwen, DeepSeek) by changing one string. The whole app is one HTML file: no build step, no server, works from a phone on location.

Why open innovation matters here: a spooky story is only as good as the people in it. Open models let a small investigator, a local historian, or a family remembering someone keep the whole tool in their own hands — no account, no server storing their case log, no company deciding which past gets remembered. The past belongs to everyone, so the tool for honoring it should too.

Respect note: this journal is for remembering people with care, not mocking the dead. Write about the living history of a place — who worked there, who loved there, who is missed there — and leave pranks and disrespect out of the log.
